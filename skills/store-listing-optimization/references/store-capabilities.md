# Store-presence and growth-tool inventory

Checked 2026-10-30. This is a decision map, not a frozen list of every console feature. Inspect the current console, official changes and app eligibility; menus evolve. Use it for a full audit, not for every narrow copy edit.

## Look at the whole customer-facing entry

Inspect the listing in the relevant countries/languages and device layouts: name/subtitle or short description, full copy, keywords where supported, category/tags, icon, screenshots, preview/video, creative placements, ratings/review themes and support/privacy links. Note mismatches between the promise and published app. Check app availability, price/purchases/ads disclosures only where they affect the stated audience or conversion hypothesis; do not change those settings as a side effect of an audit.

## Compare tools by purpose

| Goal | Apple starting points | Google Play starting points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnose discovery and return use | Analytics Sources, Engagement/retention, benchmarks | Grow overview, Statistics, listing conversion, peer groups, Android vitals |
| Match a page to an audience/campaign | Custom Product Pages | Custom store listings, targeting and groups |
| Compare randomized variants | Product Page Optimization | Localized/default listing experiments |
| Reuse compatible media | Asset Library | Asset Library |
| Promote a real timely experience | In-App Events | Promotional Content, subject to app/access eligibility |
| Seek editorial/promotional exposure | Featuring Nominations and current promotion guidance | Available featuring requests/growth recommendations and program eligibility |
| Attribute and route visitors | Campaign links and supported deep links | Supported source/UTM campaigns and deep-link tools |

These are functional counterparts, not identical products. For asset/page/experiment/event preparation and configuration, read the matching `store-screenshots` reference. Do not copy limits, metrics or eligibility across stores. An experiment must be statistically interpretable; another audience's page is not a randomized treatment. A library saves production effort; it does not itself acquire users. Featuring requests do not guarantee selection or reach.

Review additional surfaces only when relevant and available: Google's Play Shorts, pre-registration, Play Games or Play Points, for example, have different purposes and prerequisites. An already published utility app does not need a game program or pre-registration merely because the console mentions it. Apple Ads/Google Ads are separate paid acquisition options, not prerequisites for using custom pages; any spend still needs its own authorization and budget.

## Deliver a usable opportunity list

For each relevant item retain observed availability/current use, audience/problem, proposed action, prerequisites/cost, evidence and expected measurement. Classify it as act now, prepare/test later, not applicable, unavailable/not eligible or needs inspection, with a reason. Choose a small prioritized set from the actual bottleneck; do not activate every feature as a checklist. Specify untouched/uninspected areas when the audit was partial.

A follow-up can then request, for example, one audience page with localized assets and campaign measurement. Preserve that scope and confirm its actual configured/review/public state rather than reporting a recommendation as implemented.

## Current official starting points

- [Google Grow overview and use of growth tools](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16394358).
- [Google Play console changes](https://google.play/business/whats-new/): useful for discovering new sections and changed report definitions; verify each applicable feature in its operating guide.
- [Google custom listings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867158), [experiments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12053285), [Promotional Content and eligibility](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12932541).
- [Apple promotion tools and featuring](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/promote/), [Custom Product Pages](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/), [PPO](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/), [In-App Events](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/).

For metric definitions use `measurement.md`; for media mechanics use `store-screenshots`. A guide's age, a search result or a “New” badge does not establish the app's actual current capabilities.
