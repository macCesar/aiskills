# User content and DMCA

## Applicability and official sources

Sources consulted 2026-10-22; reopen at use time:

- [Copyright Office §512 overview](https://www.copyright.gov/512/): US safe harbors depend on service activity and conditions. User uploads are a reason to evaluate applicability, not proof that every app must register an agent. Distinguish hosting, caching, transmission and linking; do not substitute DMCA for other markets' notice regimes or general content moderation.
- [17 USC §512](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html#512), particularly (c), (g), (i) and (j): assess notice elements, removal/access restriction, user notification, counter-notice and restoration; repeat-infringer policy and standard technical measures are also relevant safe-harbor conditions. Under (g), the restoration window is 10–14 business days after receipt of a qualifying counter-notice unless the designated agent receives notice of the specified court action. Verify the current wording and prerequisites before operating this workflow.
- [Designated Agent Directory](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/) and [official FAQs](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html): where relevant to the safe harbor, public agent contact information and Office registration are separate steps. Check legal entity/alternate names, valid provider and agent contact fields, authorization, current fee and renewal. Designations require renewal at least every three years; confirm current requirements before filing.

## Inspection and corrections

- Discover uploads, comments, profiles, public URLs, object storage/CDN, reporting endpoints, moderation tools and existing copyright policies. Determine the entity operating the service and the safe harbor sought before recommending registration.
- Prepare an accessible notice channel and secure case intake: signature, work identification, material/location, contact information, good-faith and accuracy/authority statements. Validate completeness without treating every report as a legally valid takedown demand. Route disputed legal determinations to the authorized decision maker.
- Implement case IDs, restricted evidence access, receipt timestamps, notifications, removal/disablement across cached copies, counter-notice intake/forwarding, deadline tracking and restoration with a court-action hold. Counter-notices need the statutory identification, signature, sworn mistake/misidentification statement, contact and jurisdiction/service statements. Do not generate attestations on someone's behalf or auto-delete based solely on keywords.
- Prepare an operational repeat-infringer process and handling of standard technical measures for the verified safe-harbor scope. Ask for unresolved policy decisions rather than inventing permanent-ban thresholds. Maintain an audit trail with appropriate retention and minimized personal data.
- If agent details are missing, request the provider identity and designated agent/contact information. Prepare clearly marked drafts and configuration outside public pages; do not claim that a contact form completes registration. With sufficient tools, access and explicit filing authority, complete the external process and record directory evidence and renewal ownership. Otherwise identify exactly what remains blocked.

## Technical verification

Use synthetic notices and content in a temporary environment. Test complete/incomplete reports, delivery and access controls, content/CDN disablement, uploader notification, counter-notice forwarding and simulated restoration/hold dates. Verify directory identity, contact consistency and expiry read-only where available. Do not submit a real notice, register a fictional agent or contact a claimant as a test. Distinguish a tested intake workflow from agent registration and substantive legal qualification.
